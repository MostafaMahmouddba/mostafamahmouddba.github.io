The policy behind it

  • Before the provider accepts: the customer can cancel for free.
  • Customer cancels after acceptance: 100% refund 24h+ before, 50% between 4h and 24h, 0% under 4h.
  • Provider cancels: the customer always gets 100% back, and the provider gets a penalty.
  • Before refunding a provider-side cancellation, Ops tries to reassign the booking to another provider.
  • Provider no-show: 100% refund, a penalty, and the case goes to Operations for review.
  • Cancellation and refund go out as one notification, sent after the refund is confirmed.
