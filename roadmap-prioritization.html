Where I went against the score

  • Auto-reassign moved up. RICE rewards reach, and few orders get declined. But each decline is a lost order and an angry customer, and no other item touches the second KPI.
  • Live ETA moved down. A good score doesn't help if the provider app can't send location yet. Starting it now means the team waits half the quarter.
  • Subscriptions got a test, not a slot. Confidence of 50% on 5 person-months is a bet. A fake-door button tells us more in two weeks than a spec would.
  • Chat translation is out this quarter. Useful, but it serves neither Q1 KPI. It stays in the backlog with its score.

How I score

ReachCustomers or orders affected per quarter, from last quarter's data
Impact3 massive · 2 high · 1 medium · 0.5 low · 0.25 minimal, judged against the quarter KPIs only
Confidence100% backed by data · 80% partial data · 50% gut feeling, with steps in between
EffortPerson-months across design, dev and QA, estimated with the tech lead
ScoreReach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort

Scores get reviewed at the start of each month with the latest data. The roadmap changes only if a score moves enough to swap places.
