Post-launch Review: One-tap Rebook

Six weeks after launch, this is the review I share with the team and management: did the feature move the KPI it was built for, what did the test say, what broke on the side, and what we do next. The feature comes from the Q1 roadmap sample.

Goal: repeat-order rate 22% → 26% Test: A/B, 50/50 split, 4 weeks Full rollout: week 11 Data up to: week 12
✓

Decision: keep it at 100% and fix the provider-availability gap. Rebook lifted repeat orders by 2.5 points with a clear test result. It also raised provider declines, because customers rebook the same provider without knowing if they are free. That is the next iteration, not a reason to roll back.

What happens next

1. Show availability before rebookCheck the provider's calendar first. If they're busy, offer their next free slot or a similar provider. Target: decline rate back under 8%.
2. Move the card upA/B test the Rebook card above the home banners. Target: seen → tapped from 39% to 45%.
3. Close the gap to 26%Rebook alone gets us to about 25%. Saved addresses ships next sprint and should cover part of the rest.

What I'd do differently

  • Provider decline rate was added as a guardrail in week 2, after Ops raised it. It should have been in the PRD from day one: any feature that sends demand to one specific provider needs it.
  • The category split should have been planned before the test, not found after. Next time it goes into the test plan so the result isn't a lucky slice.
