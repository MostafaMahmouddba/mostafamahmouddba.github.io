PRD: Provider Reassignment

Moving a booking to a new service provider instead of cancelling it, in a home-services marketplace.

Author: Mostafa MahmoudPlatforms: Customer App · Provider Portal · Ops AdminStatus: Ready for grooming

1. Problem

When an assigned provider can't make it, the only option today is to cancel. The customer loses the booking, has to rebook from scratch, and the marketplace loses the order. Operations has no way to step in and save it.

Evidence

  • A meaningful share of cancellations are provider-side and happen after the booking was accepted.
  • Customers who face a provider-side cancellation are less likely to book again.
  • Ops agents already try to find a replacement manually, by phone, with no record in the system.

2. Goal & success metrics

Keep the booking alive by letting Operations move it to another qualified provider, with the customer informed and nothing lost along the way.

PrimaryShare of at-risk bookings saved through reassignment instead of cancellation
SecondaryOverall cancellation rate, and repeat-booking rate of affected customers
GuardrailNo rise in complaints or in time-to-service for reassigned bookings

3. Scope

In scopeOut of scope
Ops Admin reassigns a booking to another providerCustomer choosing a new provider themselves
Effects on the old and the new provider's portalAutomatic matching of the replacement
Customer notification and updated booking detailsChanges to pricing or commission
Audit log of every reassignmentReassigning completed or in-progress bookings

4. Business rules

  1. Reassignment is available only for bookings that are pending or accepted.
  2. A booking can be reassigned once per service.
  3. The new provider must offer the same service in the same area.
  4. The booking is auto-accepted for the new provider; no extra confirmation step.
  5. Price, date and time stay the same; commission is unchanged.
  6. The old provider loses access to the booking immediately.
  7. The customer receives one notification with the new provider's details.
  8. Every reassignment is written to an audit log: who, when, from which provider to which, and why.

5. User stories & acceptance criteria

US-01 · Ops Admin reassigns a booking

As an Operations agent, I want to move a booking to another provider, so the customer still gets served instead of the booking being cancelled.

Given a booking with status "Accepted" And it has not been reassigned before When the agent selects "Reassign" and picks an eligible provider Then the booking moves to the new provider as "Accepted" And an audit log entry is created
Given a booking that was already reassigned once When the agent opens its actions Then the "Reassign" option is not available

US-02 · Provider portal reflects the change

As a provider, I want to see bookings that are moved to or away from me, so my schedule is always accurate.

Given a booking was reassigned from Provider A to Provider B When Provider A opens their bookings Then the booking no longer appears in their active list And Provider B sees it as "Accepted" with the original date and time

US-03 · Customer is informed

As a customer, I want to know if my provider changed, so I'm not surprised on the day of the service.

Given my booking was reassigned When the reassignment is saved Then I receive one notification with the new provider's name And the booking details show the new provider

6. Rollout & measurement

  1. Release to the Operations team first, with release notes and a short walkthrough.
  2. Track the success metrics weekly against the four weeks before launch.
  3. Review the audit log for misuse, such as repeated reassignments from the same provider.
  4. Decide on phase 2 (customer-initiated reassignment) based on the results.

7. Open questions

  • Should providers who are reassigned away from repeatedly be flagged automatically?
  • Do we need a reason list for reassignment, or is free text enough for the audit log?

This is a sample written to show how I structure a PRD. The feature, rules and metrics are generic and do not describe any company's product.
