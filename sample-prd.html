PRD: Provider Reassignment

Let Operations move a booking to another provider when the assigned one drops out, instead of cancelling it.

OwnerMostafa Mahmoud
Version1.2 · Ready for grooming
PlatformsOps Admin, Provider Portal, Customer App

1Problem

When a provider drops a booking they already accepted, the only action available is to cancel and refund. The customer has to start over, and the order is lost even when another provider in the same area is free at that time.

  • ~3.5% of all bookings are cancelled by the provider after acceptance.
  • About 6 in 10 of those happen more than 2 hours before the slot, so a replacement was realistic.
  • Customers who go through a provider-side cancellation rebook about 30% less over the next 60 days.
  • Ops agents already call other providers to save these bookings, but there's no way to record it in the system.

5Business rules

  1. BR-1 Reassignment is allowed for services that are Pending or Accepted only.
  2. BR-2 Each service can be reassigned once. A second drop goes to the normal cancellation flow.
  3. BR-3 In a multi-service booking, reassignment is per service. Other services stay with their providers.
  4. BR-4 Eligible providers offer the same service, cover the customer's area and are free at the same slot.
  5. BR-5 Reassignment must happen at least 2 hours before the slot. Under 2 hours, the action is hidden.
  6. BR-6 The new provider gets the service as Accepted with no extra confirmation step.
  7. BR-7 Price, date, time and commission don't change. Payment is not touched.
  8. BR-8 The original provider loses access right away, and the drop still counts as a provider-side cancellation for penalty purposes.
  9. BR-9 A reason is mandatory and comes from a fixed list.
  10. BR-10 Every reassignment writes an audit entry: agent, time, service, from-provider, to-provider, reason.

6User stories & acceptance criteria

US-01 · Ops reassigns a service

As an Ops agent, I want to move a service to another provider, so the customer is still served.

AC-01.1 Given an Accepted service, never reassigned, more than 2 hours before its slot When the agent picks an eligible provider and a reason and confirms Then the service moves to the new provider as Accepted And an audit entry is created
AC-01.2 Given a service that was already reassigned once When the agent opens the booking Then the "Reassign" action is not shown for that service
AC-01.3 Given no provider meets the eligibility rules When the agent opens "Reassign" Then an empty state explains why And the agent can go to the normal cancellation flow

US-02 · Providers see the change

As a provider, I want my schedule to reflect bookings moved to or away from me.

AC-02.1 Given a service moved from Provider A to Provider B When Provider A opens their bookings Then the service is no longer in their active list And Provider B sees it as Accepted with the original slot and price

US-03 · Customer is informed

As a customer, I want to know if my provider changed, so I'm not surprised on the day.

AC-03.1 Given my service was reassigned When the change is saved Then I get one notification with the new provider's name And the booking details show the new provider

8Edge cases

10Decisions & risks

DecisionWhy
Auto-accept for Provider BOps already confirms by phone. An extra step adds delay and a second chance to drop.
Once per serviceAvoids bookings bouncing between providers. Ops agreed after reviewing last quarter's cases.
Ops-only in phase 1Keeps quality under control while we learn. Customer self-service is phase 2 if the primary metric holds.
Penalty still applies to Provider AWithout it, providers could drop bookings knowing Ops will cover them.

Risks

  • More load on Ops during peak hours. Mitigation: track reassignments per agent per day in week 1.
  • Reassigned customers rate lower. Mitigation: guardrail metric, review after 30 days.

11Rollout & open questions

  1. Release to Ops in one city first, with release notes and a 15-minute walkthrough.
  2. Compare metrics weekly against the 4 weeks before launch.
  3. Go to all cities if the primary metric is above 25% after 4 weeks and the guardrail holds.

Open questions

  • Should providers who are reassigned away from often get flagged automatically?
  • Do we show the customer the reason, or just the new provider?
